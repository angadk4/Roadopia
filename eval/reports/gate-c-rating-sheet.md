**1–5 in the "Fun" column**: 1 = dead boring · 2 = meh · 3 = decent · 4 = properly fun ·

a guess poisons the formula. If you get a chance to drive 2–3 of your favourites before
rating, even better. The "curv" column is the machine's current opinion (1/km score) —
**ignore it while rating**; disagreeing with it is exactly the signal we need.
